    line's full, unsplit text, ignoring cursor position entirely - text
    past the cursor just stayed put instead of moving to the new line.

    line's text there, matching normal textarea behavior. Also

    focusing it, since focusing a populated input defaults to placing
    the cursor at the end.

    original line, the text after it correctly became a new line, and
    an unrelated third line was left untouched.
